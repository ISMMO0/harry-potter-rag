# Hogwarts and the Sorting Hat
Hogwarts is a school for young witches and wizards. Its four houses are Gryffindor, Hufflepuff, Ravenclaw and Slytherin. The Sorting Hat assigns new students to a house by considering their qualities and preferences. Harry Potter, Hermione Granger and Ron Weasley belong to Gryffindor. The school's headmaster during much of Harry's education is Albus Dumbledore.
