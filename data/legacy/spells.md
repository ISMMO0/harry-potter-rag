# Defensive magic
The Patronus Charm is a defensive spell that can repel Dementors. Its incantation is Expecto Patronum. A fully formed Patronus takes an animal shape; Harry's Patronus is a stag. Remus Lupin teaches Harry this difficult spell. Expelliarmus is a disarming spell used to knock an opponent's wand away. These spells serve different defensive purposes.
