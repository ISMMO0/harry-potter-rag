# Quidditch
Quidditch is a sport played on flying broomsticks. Each team has seven players: three Chasers, two Beaters, one Keeper and one Seeker. Harry plays Seeker for Gryffindor. The Seeker tries to catch the Golden Snitch, a small winged ball. Catching it earns 150 points and ends the match. Chasers score using the Quaffle while Beaters use bats to deflect Bludgers.
