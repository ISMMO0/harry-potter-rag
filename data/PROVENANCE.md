# Corpus provenance

The active corpus is seven user-supplied Markdown summaries in `data/books/`, one for each Harry Potter novel. They are prose summaries, not PDFs, ebooks, or excerpts from the novels. The application indexes these summaries only and does not claim to contain the books.

The five original demonstration notes (`friends`, `hogwarts`, `horcruxes`, `quidditch`, and `spells`) are retained in `data/legacy/` for reference and as a fallback if `data/books/` is empty. They are not mixed into the active seven-book corpus.

The summaries should be treated as secondary, potentially AI-assisted material: factual details should be checked before presenting the corpus as authoritative. Do not add copyrighted novel text or documents you are not authorized to use.

Harry Potter names and concepts belong to their respective rights holders. This is an independent, non-commercial fan-themed portfolio project and is not official or endorsed.
